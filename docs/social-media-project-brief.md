# Curated — Brand, Content & Business Brief

Background for helping the founder of Curated create social media content and think through business ideas. Last updated 14 November 2026. Figures are real as of that date. Don't invent new ones: ask instead.

---

## 1. The product in one line

**Curated: your London, planned.** Save the spots you find on TikTok and Instagram, and Curated turns them into real plans.

### The problem it solves
People save dozens of restaurants, bars and things to do from TikTok and Instagram, then never go. The saves are scattered across apps and screenshots, with no addresses, no prices and no plan.

### What Curated does
1. **Save from anywhere.** Screenshot a TikTok or Instagram post, paste a link or a Google Maps URL, or type a name. AI reads it, finds the venue and adds the photo, rating, address and price automatically.
2. **Plan it.** Answer a few quick questions (vibe, area, budget, group size, energy) and get a timed itinerary with 3–5 stops, walking times and a cost estimate. Pick an area and find what's within a **5, 10 or 15-minute walk** to build a walking route. There's a bar crawl mode too.
3. **Do it with friends.** Connect with a 4-letter friend code, browse friends' saves, build shared bucket lists and tick places off together, see a feed of what friends are saving, and schedule visits on a calendar.

Extras:
- a swipe deck to discover venues, with a taste-match score
- an interactive map of everything you've saved
- auto-sorted lists with photo-collage covers
- celebrity-spotted badges 💫
- confetti and haptics on the moments that matter

### Where it is
- **Live** as a mobile web app (it can be installed to the home screen) at **london-app.vercel.app**.
- **Early beta:** a small group of testers (about 11 accounts). App Store and Play Store versions are planned but not built yet.
- **Free** to use right now. No business model has been chosen yet.

- **Vibe tags:** social, aesthetic, chill, foodie, hidden gems, outdoor, cultural, romantic, iconic, fancy, chaotic, solo, underground, scenic, active, affordable.

Example venues in the app (real, useful for itinerary posts): Monmouth Coffee (Borough), The Courtauld Gallery (Covent Garden), HMS Belfast, Bunhill Fields, Keats House (Hampstead), Dalston Eastern Curve Garden, EartH (Dalston), Lamb & Flag (Covent Garden), Brockwell Park, Peckham's Bussey Building, Spitalfields City Farm.

## 3. Audience

- **Primary:** Londoners aged roughly 20–35 who find places on TikTok and Instagram, love planning date nights, birthdays and weekends, and have a "London bucket list" they never get through.
- **Also:** new Londoners and students exploring the city, couples planning dates, friend groups who can never decide where to go, solo explorers, and visitors.
- **Their moments:** "what should we do this weekend?", date-night planning, birthdays, "I saved this ages ago", rainy-day plans, cheap nights out, exploring a new area.

## 4. Brand

- **Name:** Curated (also written "Curated London"). Tagline: **"Your London, planned."**
- **Look:** warm and editorial.
  - Off-white/cream backgrounds `#FAF7F2` and `#F1EDE4`
  - Near-black ink `#14140F`
  - Signature tomato red `#D9412B`
  - Accents: deep teal `#0F6B63` and olive `#726A4E`
- **Type:** Instrument Serif for headlines (elegant, magazine feel) and DM Sans for body text.
- **Voice:** a well-connected London friend. Warm, casual, specific and a bit playful. Practical tips over hype ("expect a queue, grab beans to take home"). British spelling, £ prices, real neighbourhood names. Emojis used lightly.
- **Feel:** effortless, social, a little bit "in the know". Never corporate.

## 5. Content pillars (starting ideas)

1. **"Saved it → did it."** Turn a viral TikTok spot into a real plan in the app: screen recording, then going there.
2. **Walkable itineraries.** "3 stops, 15 minutes' walk, under £30 — Shoreditch edition." One per neighbourhood.
3. **Hidden gems and themed lists.** Rainy-day London, free London, solo-date London, romantic London, late-night eats.
4. **Friends and group planning.** "Our shared bucket list: 12/30 ticked off", friend-code challenges, "you both saved this".
5. **Behind the build.** A founder diary building a London app: features, tester feedback, data milestones (for example "we just mapped 1,000 venues").
6. **Interactive and community.** "This or that" polls (a mirror of the in-app game), "drop your area and we'll plan your night", creator collaborations.

Formats: TikTok and Reels (screen recordings and on-location), Instagram carousels (itineraries, lists), Stories (polls, this-or-that), and LinkedIn for the founder and build story.

## 6. Business context & open questions

Still undecided, so these are good topics for advice and brainstorming:
- **Business model.** Options to explore: venue partnerships or featured spots, affiliate bookings (tables, tickets, experiences), premium features (unlimited AI plans, group tools), creator collaborations, sponsored curated lists, B2B for hotels or tourism.
- **Growth.** Beta-to-launch plan, invite-only codes (planned), referral loops through friend codes and shared lists, partnering with London TikTok creators.
- **Positioning against alternatives.** Google Maps saved lists, TikTok and Instagram saves, screenshots folders, and other place-saving and recommendation apps. Curated's angle is **capture from social → AI plan → do it with friends**.
- **Roadmap.** App Store and Play Store launch, event reminders, community plans (popular itineraries), more coverage (markets, museums and nightlife in some areas), and possibly expanding beyond London (the app works for any city).

When giving business advice, be practical and specific to an early-stage, solo or small-team consumer app. Flag assumptions, and don't make up market figures.
